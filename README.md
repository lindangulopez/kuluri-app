# Kuluri app

**Record once, share with control.**

Kuluri as a product, beyond the weekend prototype. The prototype built at the EU Agri-Hackathon 2026 (Team 27, Challenge 3) lives in [lindangulopez/kuluri](https://github.com/lindangulopez/kuluri); this repository is where the app grows after the hackathon.

## What Kuluri does

Buyers and public administrations increasingly ask farmers to prove fair working conditions. Kuluri turns everyday work into trustworthy evidence:

1. **Deal**: farmer and worker agree tasks, dates and pay.
2. **Record and confirm**: the farmer records the work; the worker confirms it through their own link, in their own language, or disputes it.
3. **Report**: buyers' fair-trade and responsible-sourcing requests and public-administration reports (CAP social conditionality) are filled in from that evidence, with totals only, never personal data. Mandatory compliance is always answered; beyond that, the farmer decides which buyer sees what.
4. **Profiles**: each confirmed job builds the worker's work history and the farm's track record; each side chooses what it shows.
5. **Trust as a process**: self-declared → confirmed by the farm → identity checked → right to work confirmed.

## Roadmap

Tracked on the project board "Kuluri app roadmap", in three phases:

| Phase | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Foundations** | Data model, privacy by design (DPIA), multilingual worker flow, dispute path, from prototype to maintainable code |
| **Pilot** | A small group of farms and workers, real buyer questionnaires, legal review in one country |
| **Scale** | Identity and right-to-work checks, contracts generated from deals, imports from existing time-tracking and payroll tools, links to official channels, profiles and reviews |

## Principles

- The farmer controls what leaves the farm; workers control their own history.
- Totals only for buyers and authorities; personal data never shared without a legal basis or the worker's consent.
- Build on what exists (time tracking, payroll, social audits); do not replace it.
- Open source, documented, and archived in [Software Heritage](https://www.softwareheritage.org/).
